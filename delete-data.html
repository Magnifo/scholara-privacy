Scholara — Request Data Deletion

App: Scholara (com.magnifotech.scholara) · Developer: MagnifoTech

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Use this page to request deletion of some or all of your personal data without deleting your entire account.

What you can delete yourself in the app

  • Favorites — open Favorites and remove saved scholarships or universities.
  • Your comments — delete comments you posted on a scholarship.
  • Notifications — turn off notification permissions in Android Settings, or adjust in-app notification preferences when signed in.

How to request deletion by email (keep your account)

  1. Email magnifo123@gmail.com.
  2. Subject: Scholara data deletion request.
  3. Include the Google account email you use in Scholara.
  4. List what you want deleted (examples: favorites, comments, notification history, profile display name stored in Scholara).

We will verify the request and delete the requested data while keeping your account active, unless you ask us to delete the account as well.

Data types we can delete on request

  • Favorites
  • Comments and replies you authored
  • In-app notification records for your account
  • Push notification device tokens for your account
  • Optional profile fields stored in Scholara (where applicable)

What may be retained

  • Your signed-in account identity needed to keep the account working (for example auth identifiers), unless you request full account deletion.
  • Limited security/compliance logs related to the request for up to 90 days.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that does not identify you.
  • Your Google Account itself is managed by Google, not deleted by this request.

Processing time

We aim to complete verified data deletion requests within 30 days.
